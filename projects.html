Projects & case studies

How I think, build and improve.

These are representative areas of work. They focus on the problem, the operating approach and the business outcome rather than a wall of responsibilities.

01

Customer Success operating system

Challenge: Customer handoffs, onboarding, ongoing engagement and renewals were inconsistent.

Approach: Defined ownership, lifecycle stages, health indicators, follow-up cadences, expansion triggers and renewal workflows.

Impact: Better visibility, cleaner accountability and a more scalable customer experience.

02

Email deliverability diagnostics

Challenge: Inbox placement and reputation issues were difficult to isolate.

Approach: Reviewed authentication, domain configuration, data quality, filtering behavior and sender practices.

Impact: Clear remediation plans tied to reputation protection and marketing performance.

03

Account growth & forecasting

Challenge: Risk and expansion signals were spread across systems and conversations.

Approach: Built account plans, dashboards, pipeline discipline and executive review rhythms.

Impact: Improved visibility and decision-making, with forecast accuracy gains of 30%.

The Hybrid Close

Concept: A practical sales methodology blending consultative discovery, assertive guidance and deep qualification.

Format: Story-driven frameworks for modern B2B sellers and customer-facing teams.

Status: In development.
