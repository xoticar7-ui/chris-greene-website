What Is Zero Trust?

Zero Trust is a security model that continuously verifies access instead of trusting someone simply because they are inside the network.

By Chris J. Greene · Updated August 2026

In plain English

Zero Trust is like an airport. Getting through the front door does not let you board every plane. Identity, destination, device, and permission are checked again where they matter.

Technical idea

Zero Trust emphasizes explicit verification, least privilege, strong identity, device posture, segmentation, telemetry, and the assumption that a breach may already exist.

Why it matters

Modern users, applications, devices, and cloud services are spread everywhere. A trusted internal network boundary is no longer enough.
