Why Network Segmentation Matters

Network segmentation limits how systems communicate and reduces the damage one compromised device can cause.

By Chris J. Greene · Updated August 2026

In plain English

Segmentation is like putting fire doors inside a building. A problem in one room should not spread freely through every floor.

Technical idea

VLANs, subnets, firewalls, access-control lists, identity policies, and microsegmentation can separate users, servers, IoT, cameras, guests, AV, and sensitive systems.

Why it matters

Segmentation improves security, performance, troubleshooting, compliance, and the reliability of automation and AV systems.
